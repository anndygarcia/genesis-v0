        How it works

        From PDF to walkable home, in three steps.

        1. 01

          Upload

          Drop in any 2D plan: PDF, PNG, JPEG, DWG, DXF. Genesis normalizes and parses the drawing.

        2. 02

          Reconstruct

          Vision models detect walls, doors, windows, and rooms. The geometry engine extrudes them into a parametric 3D model.

        3. 03

          Walk through

          Open the viewer in any browser. Measure, screenshot, share a link with your client or contractor.

        Roadmap

        Shipping, in order.

        • v0 · today

          Interactive 3D viewer

          Parametric homes from procedural plans. Orbit + walk mode. Room labels with measurements.

        • v1 · next

          Real-plan ingestion

          Upload a PDF / DWG and run wall, door, and window detection. Geometry engine reconstructs the actual plan.

        • v2

          Material library & textures

          Brick, stucco, wood, stone — pick materials per surface. AI-detected from reference photos.

        • v3

          Export

          GLB for rendering, IFC for BIM, PDF dimensioned drawings for permitting.
